## Decision and scope
Keep standalone offers C01 $299, C03 $249, C04 $499, C08 $149 CAD as indicative entry prices; actual scope, availability, taxes, third-party fees and acceptance confirmed before agreement. C02/C05/C06/C07 quote individually. Estimated delivery intervals are hypotheses, not commitments. Do not imply $499 covers a complete custom website or $149 covers all repairs. No forced bundles. Full catalog remains available in V7. Design preview emphasizes outcome routes, selectable industries, deliverables, exclusions, handover, optional support and no outcome guarantees. 
## Remaining validation
Recruit 20 actual target buyers to test the Shoreline UI in English/Persian, with permission. Ask them to find a relevant service, explain its deliverables, identify price/exclusions, find consultation and compare alternatives; record task completion, objections and consent. Do not report recruitment, interview, conversion or satisfaction as achieved until evidence exists. Need actual mobile browser testing and pricing margin validation.
