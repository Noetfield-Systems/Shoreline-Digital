# Quote-to-Cash — V3
References applied: HubSpot quote management https://knowledge.hubspot.com/quotes/manage-quotes ; Stripe Checkout server-side payment status https://docs.stripe.com/payments/existing-customers ; BHP Persian positioning https://bhpsolution.com/persian-campaign ; CRTC consent evidence https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm .

Operator: create a quote for a stored request; enter actual scope, deliverables, commercial terms, applicable tax treatment, CAD total/deposit and expiration. Share the private bearer link with the intended customer only. Acceptance stores the supplied name, timestamp and immutable scope hash; it is not identity-verified e-signature. Invoice amounts are generated from the accepted quote in cents. Internal invoice records are not statutory tax invoices. Do not enter a tax number or merchant identity that does not belong to Shoreline.

Payment: configure SHORELINE_STRIPE_KEY (live secret), SHORELINE_STRIPE_ACCOUNT (the authorized independent merchant), SHORELINE_PUBLIC_URL (HTTPS origin). No shared merchant by default. On checkout the Stripe account identity is checked. Checkout session creation has an invoice-scoped idempotency key. Operator uses Verify Stripe payment to retrieve session and check live mode, currency, exact amount, status and invoice metadata. A redirect is not proof of payment. Bank transfers can be recorded by operator only after matching actual bank evidence and amount; they appear separately as bank-attested, not processor verified. No automatic webhooks, refunds or settlement/fee reconciliation yet. Expired checkout requires operator handling; do not create a new payment until reconciliation. Never expose the Stripe key. No external payment API was exercised in local tests.

Radar: record a real source URL, observation and supporting evidence, relevant service, public business contact, outreach basis, owner and dated next step. Research-only records may have unknown basis. No automatic outreach or scraping. CASL requires applicable consent, identification and unsubscribe; a public address alone is not blanket permission. No invented opportunities included.

Benchmark delivery template: client goal; scope and date; direct competitors and URLs; observed positioning, public prices, review themes with source and date; website and conversion observations; evidence versus hypothesis; prioritized recommendations with cost/effort and owner; optional implementation quote. Do not imply access to competitors' private sales or analytics. Deliver a complete standalone analysis regardless of follow-on work.
