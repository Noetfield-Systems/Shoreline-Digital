04 / Good to know

Before you book.

A straightforward approach to practical help.

How much will my service cost?

Starting prices are shown in Canadian dollars. Final scope, travel, materials and applicable taxes are confirmed before you commit.

Can I request help today or tomorrow?

Choose your preferred date in the request. It is a preference, not a guaranteed appointment; availability must be confirmed.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes. Web, AI and software support can often be delivered remotely. Business website and software support can be arranged in person.

Do I need to pay before requesting help?

No. Preparing a request is free. Any deposit or payment arrangement is agreed separately after scope and availability are confirmed.

What if my task is not listed?

Select Custom request. Describe the result you need and any relevant constraints. Regulated, unsafe or specialist work may require a qualified provider.

Is my information sent when I build a request?

In this version, the request is prepared in your browser. If a contact endpoint has not been connected, you can copy or download it; it is not sent or booked automatically.
